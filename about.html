OUR INFRASTRUCTURE

State-of-the-Art
Machinery Setup

By investing in the latest cutting and printing technology, we ensure precise cuts, vibrant colors, and unparalleled durability for every board we make.

CO2 Laser Cutting

High-precision laser technology used for cutting acrylic sheets with perfectly polished, crystal-clear edges for 3D lettering.

CNC Router

Heavy-duty computerized routing used to carve out tough materials like ACP, WPC, and MDF boards with absolute accuracy.

Vinyl Printing

Wide-format eco-solvent printers delivering vibrant, high-resolution graphics for glow signs, flex boards, and window wraps.

Plotter Cutting

Dedicated vinyl cutting plotters used to create sharp, custom-colored decals, branding stickers, and frosted glass films.

FABRICATION PROCESS

How We Build The Final Good

01

Design & Vectoring

We convert your logo into precise vector files, determining exact scaling, material layers, and LED mapping before production begins.

02

Precision Cutting

Using our CO2 Lasers and CNC Routers, the raw acrylic and ACP sheets are cut to absolute millimeter precision based on the digital design.

03

Assembly & Wiring

Our fabricators assemble the 3D structures, applying premium vinyl if required, and securely wire the waterproof LED modules internally.

04

Quality Check

Every board undergoes a strict illumination and structural integrity test to ensure no light leaks and maximum durability before dispatch.
